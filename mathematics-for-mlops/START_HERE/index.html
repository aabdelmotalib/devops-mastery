    1. WHAT & WHY

2. CORE CONCEPTS
   ↓ Definition in plain language
   ↓ Intuitive explanation
   ↓ Real examples

3. WORKED EXAMPLES
   ↓ Step-by-step solutions
   ↓ No skipped steps
   ↓ Real scenarios

   ↓ 10 practice questions (answers included)

    💡 Key Insights

    Module 1: The Monitoring Lens

    Small percentages on large scales matter. Trends matter more than snapshots.

    Module 2: The Pipeline Lens

    Data flows through functions. Order matters. Inputs/outputs must match.

    Module 3: The Learning Lens

    Models improve by walking downhill. Gradient tells direction. Learning rate tells speed.
