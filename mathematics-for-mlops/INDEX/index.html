    🎓 Teaching Philosophy

    This curriculum enforces:

    1. No topic dependencies left unexplained - You won't encounter a symbol or concept you haven't already learned
    2. Why comes first - Every topic answers: "Where is this used in real MLOps?"
    3. Concrete over abstract - Real dashboards, pipelines, and training curves
    4. Operational focus - Everything ties to decisions and actions
    5. Slow learning - Better to understand deeply than memorize broadly
    6. Patience - Difficulty is normal; confusion is feedback
