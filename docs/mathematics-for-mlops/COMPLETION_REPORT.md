## 📚 What's in Each Module

### **Module 1: Absolute Basics**
Teaches learners to understand and interpret numbers in production systems.

**Topics:**
- Numbers and signs
- Magnitude (absolute size)
- Ratios and percentages (15+ examples)
- Orders of magnitude
- Reading charts and dashboards

**Worked Examples:** 2 detailed walkthroughs
**Practice Questions:** 10 (easy → medium → hard)
**MLOps Reality Check:** 5 real failure modes

**Key Takeaway:** 
*Understand that percentages on large numbers matter more than the percentage itself. Recognize trends before they become crises. Read dashboards correctly.*

---

### **Module 2: Functions and Mappings**
Teaches learners how data transforms through pipelines and why order matters.

**Topics:**
- What a function is (definition + intuition)
- Inputs (domain), outputs (range)
- Function composition (chaining)
- Linear functions
- Non-linear functions
- Piecewise functions

**Worked Examples:** 3 detailed walkthroughs
**Practice Questions:** 10 (easy → medium → hard)
**MLOps Reality Check:** 5 real failure modes

**Key Takeaway:**
*Pipelines are chains of functions. Order matters. Match input domains to function expectations. Understand when behavior is linear vs. non-linear.*

---

### **Module 3: Derivatives and Gradients**
Teaches learners how models learn through gradient descent and why it matters.

**Topics:**
- What a derivative is (local rate of change)
- Gradients (multi-variable derivatives)
- Gradient descent algorithm
- Learning rate (step size)
- Convergence (when to stop)
- Non-convexity (local minima problem)

**Worked Examples:** 3 detailed walkthroughs
**Practice Questions:** 10 (easy → medium → hard)
**MLOps Reality Check:** 5 real failure modes

**Key Takeaway:**
*Model training is walking downhill on a loss landscape. Gradient tells you direction, learning rate tells you step size. Convergence happens when gradient is near zero.*

## 💪 What Learners Can Now Do

After completing these three modules, a learner can:

1. **Read dashboards and understand metrics**
   - Spot real problems vs. noise
   - Calculate percentage change and trends
   - Predict when something will hit a threshold

2. **Understand data pipelines**
   - Trace data transformations
   - Identify where functions are composed
   - Debug mismatches between functions

3. **Understand model training**
   - Read loss curves and interpret them
   - Diagnose bad learning rates
   - Know when to stop training
   - Catch convergence issues

4. **Monitor production models**
   - Track loss and performance metrics
   - Detect data drift (loss increasing)
   - Know when retraining is needed

5. **Explain these concepts** (because they're grounded in intuition, not memorization)

## 🎓 Philosophy Applied Throughout

This curriculum enforces:

**Zero topic dependencies:** Nothing is assumed to be known in advance

**Operational focus:** Every concept connects to real MLOps work

**Slow pace:** Better to understand one concept deeply than memorize many

**Concrete over abstract:** Real examples before general principles

**Why matters:** Understanding *why* is more important than memorizing *how*
