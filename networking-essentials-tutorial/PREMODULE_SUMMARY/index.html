    Premodule Created: Networking Basics A-to-Z

    Summary

    I've created a comprehensive premodule for your networking-essentials-tutorial that covers fundamental networking concepts from A to Z. This serves as an excellent general starting point for anyone learning networking.

    What Was Created

    File: docs/00-networking-basics-a-to-z.md

    Duration: 2-3 hours | Level: Beginner | Prerequisites: None

    A complete A-to-Z introduction covering 26 core networking concepts:

    • A: Addressing & IP (IPv4, IPv6, CIDR notation)
    • B: Bandwidth & Bytes (throughput, latency)
    • C: Clients & Connections
    • D: Datagrams & DNS (with resolution process)
    • E: Ethernet & Encapsulation (layering model)
    • F: Firewalls & Flow (rules, connection tracking)
    • G: Gateways & Routing
    • H: Headers & Hops (TTL, hop limits)
    • I: Internet & Interfaces (network interfaces, addressing)
    • J: Jumbo Frames (MTU considerations)
    • K: Keyed Sockets (4-tuple connections)
    • L: Latency & Layers (OSI model)
    • M: MAC & Multiplexing (physical addressing, HTTP/2)
    • N: Networks & Namespaces (Docker isolation)
    • O: Open Systems & OSI Model (7-layer reference)
    • P: Ports & Protocols (well-known ports, HTTP, TCP, UDP)
    • Q: QoS & Queue (traffic prioritization, backpressure)
    • R: Routing & Requests (HTTP methods, routing table)
    • S: Sockets & Servers (connection states, listening)
    • T: TCP/IP & Transport (3-way handshake, flow control)
    • U: UDP & User Data (fast, unreliable delivery)
    • V: Virtual & VLAN (virtual networks, logical grouping)
    • W: Wireshark & Web (packet sniffing, HTTP/HTTPS)
    • X: X.509 Certificates (encryption, HTTPS)
    • Y: Yet Another Protocol (standards, REST, RFCs)
    • Z: Zero-Knowledge (authentication, authorization, encryption)
